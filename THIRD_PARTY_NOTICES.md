## Geographic map data

The English Channel, Strait of Gibraltar, and Taiwan Strait maps use public-domain Natural Earth 1:50,000,000 land data ([terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)). Official maintained repository: `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector`; pinned revision: `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`; file: `geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson`; original SHA-256: `e874b27a51d146452be360cafb3cc50c86001074a67d534113e6534682f9826b`. This project crops, applies an equirectangular projection, and samples the data into game land/water grids. Offline data: `src/map-geography.js`; provenance: `assets/maps/geography-source.json`; reproducible tool: `tools/build-geographic-maps.cjs`. Bases, mines, and objectives are original fictional placements. The map scale does not mean 50-metre accuracy; the data does not include real military facilities or terrain elevation.
